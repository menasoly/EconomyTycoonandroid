# Product Vision

## Summary

EconomyTycoon is a business and economic simulation for mobile in which the player builds companies, runs production and logistics, trades in markets and competes against AI companies that obey the same economic rules as the player.

## Pillars

1. Economic depth that stays fun to play.
2. Emergent outcomes from simple, composable rules rather than scripts.
3. Decisions with measurable, explainable consequences.
4. Interconnected industries driven by data, not per-industry code.
5. AI competitors that play by the player's rules.
6. Deterministic simulation: same seed and same inputs give the same world.
7. Offline-first single player; multiplayer is optional and separate.
8. Management UI that is fast and readable on phones and tablets.

## Audience

- Casual players: guided onboarding, advisors, sensible defaults, optional automation.
- Advanced players: manual control over pricing, production lines, contracts, financing and strategy.

## MVP scope (vertical slice)

- One region, a small set of industries (agriculture, food processing, retail).
- Player company plus a handful of AI competitors.
- Raw material market, finished goods market, simple consumer demand.
- Factory, production recipes, inventory, employees (aggregate), loans.
- Double-entry ledger with balance sheet and income statement.
- Save/load and deterministic replay of a session.

## Long-term scope

Additional industries and regions, trade and tariffs, capital markets, research, government and macroeconomics, scenarios and campaigns, rich map visualization, optional cloud saves and multiplayer.

## Non-goals

- No real-money gameplay or real financial products. All currency and assets are fictional.
- No pay-to-win mechanics; paid content must not silently change shared competitive outcomes.
- No dependence on a network connection for single-player.
